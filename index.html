About

For fourteen years I've operated the power and cooling behind a global IT footprint — data centers, call centers, and COLOs across multiple countries — targeting six-nines availability from a small team. I've led resiliency upgrades, delivered new-site build-outs, and managed major equipment replacements and decommissions end to end.

What sets my work apart is the second half: I'm the sole administrator of our Nlyte DCIM platform, and — largely self-taught — I design and ship the custom software that monitors and manages the environment. I replaced a three-vendor alerting pipeline with a platform I built in .NET, write the SQL behind the DCIM, and build Python tooling for data and reporting. If it involves keeping critical infrastructure alive, or automating the parts that shouldn't need a human, that's my lane.
